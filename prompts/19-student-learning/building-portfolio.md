## 🎯 Prompt

```
Help me build a developer portfolio.

My skills: [DESCRIBE]
Career goal: [DESCRIBE: frontend dev, backend, ML, etc.]
Experience level: [STUDENT / CAREER CHANGER / JUNIOR]

Portfolio strategy:

**Projects to build** (3-5 is enough):
1. **Showcase project**: your best work, production-quality
   - Has real users or compelling demo
   - Clean code, good README
   - Deployed and working
2. **Breadth project**: shows different skills
3. **Domain project**: specific to your target role

**GitHub profile**:
4. Pinned repositories: your best 6
5. Profile README: who you are + skills + contact
6. Regular commits: green contribution graph
7. Each repo: good README + live demo link

**Personal website**:
8. About: who you are, what you do
9. Projects: screenshots + descriptions + links
10. Blog: optional, but differentiates you
11. Contact: easy to reach

For my background:
- 3 specific project ideas to build
- Which technologies to use
- Deploy targets
```

## 💡 Tips & Variations

- Works best with **Claude Sonnet 4** or **Fable 5**
- Adjust `[BRACKETED PLACEHOLDERS]` to your specific use case
