## 🎯 Prompt

```
Give me personalized developer career advice.

Current situation: [DESCRIBE: student / bootcamp grad / self-taught / career changer]
Skills: [LIST YOUR SKILLS]
Goal role: [DESCRIBE: frontend, backend, full-stack, ML, etc.]
Location: [CITY OR REMOTE]
Timeline: [WHEN YOU WANT TO GET A JOB]

Personalized roadmap:
1. **Skill gaps**: what you need to learn for your target role
2. **Portfolio**: what projects to build
3. **Job applications**: where to look, how many to send
4. **Resume**: what to include at your level
5. **Interview prep**: what to study

General advice:
6. **Networking**: LinkedIn, meetups, online communities
7. **Job boards**: roles that accept your level
8. **Company types**: startup vs big tech vs agency
9. **Negotiating**: even junior devs can negotiate
10. **Red flags**: companies that will waste your time

Candid assessment of my situation and realistic expectations.
```
