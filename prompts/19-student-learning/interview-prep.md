## 🎯 Prompt

```
Help me prepare for technical interviews.

Roles I'm applying for: [DESCRIBE: frontend, backend, full-stack, ML]
Timeline: [HOW MANY WEEKS TO PREPARE]
Strength/weakness: [DESCRIBE YOUR STRENGTHS AND GAPS]

8-week preparation plan:

**Weeks 1-2 — Data Structures**:
- Arrays, strings, hash maps
- Linked lists, stacks, queues
- Trees and graphs

**Weeks 3-4 — Algorithms**:
- BFS, DFS
- Binary search
- Two pointers, sliding window
- Dynamic programming basics

**Weeks 5-6 — Practice**:
- 2-3 LeetCode problems per day
- Mix: 70% medium, 30% hard
- Review: go back to failed problems

**Week 7 — System Design**:
- Design URL shortener, Uber, Twitter
- Practice whiteboarding

**Week 8 — Behavioral + Mock**:
- STAR method for behavior questions
- 2 mock interviews with a peer

Daily resources + strategies for staying consistent.
```
